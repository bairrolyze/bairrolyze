Our Story

I built this because my family needed it

A few years ago, we were searching for our first family home in Portugal. We weren't just looking for the right house — we needed the right neighbourhood.

"Will my children be able to walk to school? What if there's an emergency — how far is the nearest hospital? Is public transport good enough that we don't need a second car? Will there be parks nearby for weekends?"

We spent weeks opening Google Maps tab after tab, calculating walking distances one by one, reading local forums, cross-referencing apps. It was exhausting — and we still weren't confident we had the complete picture.

So I built HomeScope. Now I can paste any address and within seconds see every school, hospital, transit stop, and park within walking distance — with an AI-generated summary and a single score out of 100.

A decision that used to take our family three weeks now takes thirty seconds. We want every family to have that clarity.

See your entire neighborhood profile at a glance with our signature radial visualization. Every category is color-coded and weighted around a central score — instantly revealing strengths and weaknesses.

Color-Coded Categories Transport, Education, Shopping, Healthcare, Safety, Recreation — each with its own signature color

Interactive & Touch-Friendly Tap any segment to drill down into that category's detailed breakdown

🔮 Time Machine Analysis

See how your neighborhood transforms throughout the day. Slide through morning, afternoon, evening, and night to understand transit frequency, shop hours, safety levels, and activity patterns at different times.

🌅
Time-of-Day Scoring Transit at 7 AM vs 11 PM? Shop availability midday vs midnight? See real-time changes

Transit Schedule Integration Live analysis of bus/metro frequency - know if you'll wait 5 minutes or 45 at different times
🌙
Night Safety & Activity Understand which areas stay lively vs. quiet after dark, lighting conditions, and foot traffic

🧬 Area DNA Fingerprint

Every neighborhood has a unique character — a "DNA" that defines what it's like to actually live there. Our radar chart instantly reveals whether an area is transport-rich but school-poor, or healthcare-heavy but lacking recreation.

🔍
Instant Pattern Recognition Identify neighborhood "shapes" — suburban family hubs vs. urban transit zones vs. balanced mixed-use districts

🎯
