This Privacy Policy explains how [LEGAL ENTITY NAME — e.g. Tellura, Lda.] (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information in connection with the Bairrolyze mobile application (the “App”). The short version

  • No account or sign-in required.
  • No advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party trackers.
  • The address you look up is sent to our backend and mapping providers only to generate a neighbourhood score — never to identify you.
  • Your search history and saved places stay on your device, not on our servers.
  • Purchases are handled by the app store and RevenueCat — we never see your card details.

1. Information we process

Location & address data

When you search an address — or grant location permission and use “use my current location” — the App sends that address or coordinates to our backend to look up nearby amenities and calculate a Location Score. It is used only to fulfil your request and is not tied to a persistent identifier. Granting location is optional; you can type an address instead and revoke the permission anytime in device settings.

Data stored on your device

Your recent searches, saved places, and app preferences are stored only on your device and are not transmitted to us. Deleting the App removes this data.

Purchases (Bairrolyze Pro, if used)

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by the Apple App Store / Google Play and by RevenueCat. They process a purchase token/receipt to validate your entitlement. We do not receive or store your full payment card number. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Technical data

Standard network information (such as your IP address) is necessarily processed by our hosting provider and the third-party APIs below to deliver a response. We do not use it to identify you or for tracking.

We do not knowingly collect your name, email, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or health/financial data.

3. How we use information

Only to: geocode your query and calculate the Location Score; generate the amenity breakdown and summary you requested; validate paid features; and operate, secure, and debug the service. We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for advertising.

4. Legal basis (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Performance of your request / contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — to score the address you ask about and deliver purchased features.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — to keep the service secure and working.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — for device location access, withdrawable anytime in settings.

5. Data retention

On-device data remains until you delete it or uninstall the App. Address lookups may be cached temporarily to improve performance; caches expire automatically and are not used to identify you. We do not maintain user-level profiles.

6. Your rights

Under the EU/UK GDPR and similar laws you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your data, and to object to processing. Because the App has no accounts and stores history only on your device, you can exercise most of these directly by clearing data in the App or uninstalling it. For anything else, contact us below. You may also complain to your data protection authority — in Portugal, the CNPD.

7. Children’s privacy

Bairrolyze is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

8. International transfers

Some providers above (e.g. OpenAI, RevenueCat) may process data outside the EEA/UK. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.
